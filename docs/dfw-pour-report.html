FAQ

Where does the data come from?

The Texas Alcoholic Beverage Commission's public "Pending Original New Primary and Subordinate License Application(s)" dataset on the Texas Open Data Portal (data.texas.gov). We filter and clean it. DFW Pour Report is not affiliated with or endorsed by TABC. Data is provided as-is and may contain errors. A pending application doesn't guarantee a permit will be issued or that the business will open.

Are you new?

Yes. We're a small, new service with no track record yet, so we keep it simple: no contracts, cancel anytime.

Refunds?

If your first list isn't useful, email us within 7 days and we'll refund it.

Can I cold-email or call these businesses?

You're responsible for following the law when you contact anyone, including CAN-SPAM for email (identify yourself, include a postal address, honor opt-outs) and the TCPA and Do-Not-Call rules for calls and texts. Mail and in-person visits are the simplest options.
