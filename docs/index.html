FAQ

Where does the data come from?

The Texas Comptroller's public list of active sales tax permit holders, which is updated regularly. We pull it, keep the new DFW outlets, and filter and label them.

How many leads a week?

It varies with state filings. Recent weeks have had a few hundred filtered DFW leads, but we don't guarantee a number.

Can't I get this data myself?

Yes, it's public. You're paying for the weekly pull, the DFW filtering, chain removal, trade tagging and sales angles, so you don't have to do it.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. Email storefrontscout@mail.grokbot.com and we'll cancel before your next billing date.

Refunds?

If your first list isn't useful, email us within 7 days and we'll refund it.

Is it OK to contact these businesses?

They're businesses listed in public records. Use them for business-to-business outreach only, and follow CAN-SPAM, Telephone Consumer Protection Act and Do-Not-Call rules.
